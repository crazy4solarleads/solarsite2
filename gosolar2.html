How a Solar PPA Works in New Jersey

A Solar Power Purchase Agreement allows New Jersey homeowners to go solar without purchasing panels. Instead of paying unpredictable utility rates, you pay a fixed or lower rate for the solar energy produced on your roof.

Key PPA Education Points

  • No upfront installation cost
  • You only pay for the electricity produced
  • Maintenance and monitoring are included
  • Lower cost per kWh than most NJ utilities
  • Designed for long-term energy stability

Who Chooses a Solar PPA?

  • Homeowners with rising electric bills
  • People who don’t want to buy solar panels
  • Homes needing roof, tree removal, or upgrades
  • Families planning to stay long-term
  • Customers wanting predictable energy costs

Utility rates in New Jersey have increased steadily due to fuel costs, grid upgrades, and higher demand. Homeowners served by PSE&G, Atlantic City Electric, and JCP&L have seen noticeable year-over-year bill increases.

Why Solar Helps Offset Rate Increases
