⚡ Why New Jersey Homeowners Are Switching to Solar

Many NJ homeowners are paying 24–32¢ per kWh through utilities like PSEG, Atlantic City Electric, and JCP&L. These rates have increased repeatedly and show no signs of slowing down.

A solar Power Purchase Agreement (PPA) allows you to switch how you pay for electricity — without taking on equipment costs.

☀️ 3 Key Benefits of a Solar PPA

  • $0 upfront: No cost for panels, installation, or maintenance
  • Predictable pricing: Designed to beat rising NJ utility rates
  • Hassle-free: System monitoring, service, and repairs handled for you

You pay for the solar energy produced — not the equipment.
