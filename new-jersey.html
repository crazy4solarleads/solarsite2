Why New Jersey Is a Strong Solar Market

New Jersey has a long history of supporting residential solar. Between high electric rates and solid production, many homeowners see meaningful savings and long-term price protection by going solar.

Common NJ utilities switching to solar:

  • PSEG – large customer base and rising rates
  • Atlantic City Electric (ACE) – high cost per kWh in South Jersey
  • JCP&L – great solar production potential
  • Orange & Rockland – smaller territory, good roofs

Typical NJ cost comparison:

Utility: ~24–32¢ per kWh
Solar: often ~15–19¢ per kWh depending on usage, roof, and program.

New Jersey Solar FAQ

Does solar really work well in New Jersey?
Yes. NJ gets solid sun hours throughout the year and has strong solar adoption. Many neighborhoods already have solar on multiple homes.
Can I qualify with $0 upfront?
Most homeowners do, as long as the roof is in reasonable condition and the home meets basic program requirements. When you request a proposal, I’ll review your roof and usage to confirm.
What if I need a new roof or want a battery?
We can look at options that include roof work and battery backup (like Tesla Powerwall 3) so you have one simple, bundled solution.
