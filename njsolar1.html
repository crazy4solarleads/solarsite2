Electricity Rates in New Jersey Are Rising — And Homeowners Are Feeling It

Across New Jersey, homeowners served by PSE&G, Atlantic City Electric, and JCP&L have seen consistent increases in electricity rates over the past several years. Supply costs, infrastructure upgrades, fuel volatility, and growing demand on the electric grid have all contributed to higher monthly utility bills.

Many New Jersey households are now paying significantly more per kilowatt-hour than they did just a few years ago — and most industry projections show that utility rates are unlikely to go down.

Important: Utility companies control pricing. Homeowners have no protection from future increases unless they switch to an alternative energy solution like solar.

PSE&G, Atlantic City Electric, and JCP&L: What Homeowners Are Experiencing

PSE&G Customers

PSE&G customers throughout North and Central New Jersey have experienced rate increases tied to supply charges and grid modernization programs. Many homeowners are surprised to learn that the majority of their bill comes from supply — not usage.

Atlantic City Electric Customers

In South Jersey, Atlantic City Electric customers often see seasonal spikes during summer months, especially with air conditioning usage. These increases compound year after year, making long-term budgeting difficult.

JCP&L Customers

JCP&L customers across New Jersey face similar challenges, with rising delivery charges and supply costs that fluctuate with the energy market.

Regardless of provider, the trend is the same: higher bills and less control.

Why New Jersey Homeowners Are Choosing a Solar PPA

A Solar Power Purchase Agreement (PPA) allows homeowners to switch from buying electricity from the utility to buying clean solar energy produced on their own roof — without purchasing the system.

  • No upfront cost to install solar panels
  • Lower cost per kWh compared to utility rates
  • Predictable energy pricing
  • No maintenance or repair responsibility
  • Protection against future utility rate hikes
