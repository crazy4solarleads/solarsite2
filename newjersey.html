How New Jersey Solar Works for PSEG, ACE, JCP&L and O&R Customers

New Jersey is one of the strongest solar markets in the country. High electric rates, solid sun exposure, and mature interconnection rules make it a great place for homeowners to lock in predictable energy costs with a solar PPA or lease through 1One Smart Home Solar PPA.

Common NJ utilities going solar:

  • PSEG: large service area with strong savings potential on high per-kWh rates
  • Atlantic City Electric (ACE): South Jersey coastal communities with some of the highest bills
  • JCP&L: suburban and rural areas with excellent roof space and sun exposure
  • Orange & Rockland (O&R): smaller pockets where solar can still make a big impact

Benefits of a solar PPA in New Jersey

  • Lower, more predictable cost per kWh compared to volatile utility rates
  • No large upfront payment for panels, inverters, or labor
  • Professional design, permitting, installation, and monitoring all handled for you
  • Ability to pair solar with Tesla Powerwall 3 for backup power during outages

Is my New Jersey roof a good fit for solar?

Good solar roofs in New Jersey are typically:

  • Facing south, east, or west (or a combination)
  • Not heavily shaded by trees or neighboring buildings
  • In reasonable condition, or scheduled for replacement as part of the project

When you request a custom proposal, I’ll look at your roof using satellite imagery and design software, then build a layout that’s realistic for your specific home.

New Jersey Solar FAQ

Do I need to pay anything upfront to go solar in NJ?
Many New Jersey homeowners qualify for $0-down solar PPA or lease options. Your exact plan will depend on your roof condition, credit, and utility usage. I’ll walk you through the options based on your situation.
What happens if I sell my New Jersey home?
Most solar agreements can be transferred to the buyer at closing. We’ll go over the transfer steps so you can confidently explain the solar benefits to future buyers and your real estate agent.
Can I add a battery like Tesla Powerwall 3?
In many cases, yes. Some homeowners bundle solar and Powerwall together from day one, while others start with solar and add storage later. 1One Smart Home Solar PPA can help you see how battery backup fits into your long-term plan.
