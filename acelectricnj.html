Why Atlantic City Electric Customers Are Switching to Solar

Atlantic City Electric serves a wide coastal region in South Jersey, including towns like Galloway, Egg Harbor Township, Atlantic City, Pleasantville, Mays Landing, Cape May, and more. Many homeowners in these areas have seen their ACE bills climb due to higher per-kWh rates and seasonal usage.

Typical ACE vs solar cost comparison

  • ACE utility power: often in the 26–34¢ per kWh range
  • Solar PPA: commonly in the 15–19¢ per kWh range, depending on the home

Instead of paying the utility whatever rate they decide to charge, an ACE customer with solar through 1One Smart Home Solar PPA pays a predictable rate for every kilowatt-hour the system produces.

South Jersey homes that tend to be a great fit

  • Single-family homes with a mostly unobstructed roof (limited tree shading)
  • Roofs facing south, southeast, southwest, or west
  • Homes with consistently high ACE bills, especially over $180–$200/month

When you request a custom ACE proposal, I’ll look at your roof using satellite imagery, factor in your coastal weather patterns, and design a system that works with your specific property.

Atlantic City Electric Solar FAQ

Is solar worth it for ACE customers near the shore?
For many ACE customers, yes. Coastal South Jersey gets strong sun exposure, and ACE rates can be high. A well-designed solar PPA can reduce your effective cost per kWh and protect you from future rate increases, especially if you use a lot of power for AC in summer.
What about storms and outages?
Regular grid-tied solar will shut off when the grid goes down, but pairing your system with Tesla Powerwall 3 can keep essential loads (or your whole home, depending on design) running during ACE outages. We can include backup power in your proposal if you’d like.
Do I need a perfect roof to qualify?
Not necessarily. If your roof is in poor condition, we can explore options that bundle a roof replacement with your solar project so everything is handled in one plan. I’ll look at your roof condition as part of the custom review.
