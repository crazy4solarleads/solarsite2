Main Panel Upgrade Explained

Your main electrical panel is the heart of your home’s power system. If your panel is outdated, overloaded, or not compatible with solar, battery backup, or future electrical upgrades, a main panel upgrade may be needed.


A panel upgrade can help support solar panels, battery storage, EV chargers, home additions, and safer electrical capacity. 1One Smart Home helps homeowners review the full project picture so the right solution is built around the home — not just one product.
