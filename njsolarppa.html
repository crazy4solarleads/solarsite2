Why New Jersey Homeowners Choose a $0-Down Solar PPA Instead of Staying 100% with the Utility Company

This page is a deeper educational guide to help New Jersey homeowners understand how a solar Power Purchase Agreement (PPA) works, why it can beat traditional utility bills, and how upgrades like a new roof, electrical panel work, and tree removal can sometimes be rolled into one project with $0 upfront.

Core Benefits of a New Jersey Solar PPA

A solar PPA in New Jersey lets you lock in a predictable price for the power your rooftop system produces, instead of being at the mercy of your utility’s rate changes. You don’t buy the panels — you simply pay for the energy they generate, often at a lower cost per kWh.

  • ✔
    $0 upfront installation. For qualifying homeowners, there is no large cash payment to start. The solar company designs, installs and maintains the system.
  • ✔
    Beat high NJ electric rates. Many households see their effective utility rate in the 20–25¢/kWh range or higher. The goal of a PPA is to offer a lower price per kWh.
  • ✔
    Professional maintenance included. Monitoring, repairs and performance checks are typically handled for you over the life of the agreement.
  • ✔
    Protection from future rate hikes. Instead of guessing what the utility might charge in 5–10 years, you have a known structure for your energy costs.
  • ✔
    Option to add Tesla Powerwall 3. Many homeowners combine a solar PPA with battery storage for backup power during outages.

$0 Upfront Possibilities: New Roof, Electrical Upgrade & Tree Removal

One of the most powerful aspects of working with 1One Smart Home Solar PPA is the ability to look at the whole home, not just the panels. In many cases, we can explore programs where the following upgrades are considered as part of the project:

  • New Roof: If your roof is older or in poor condition, it may make sense to replace it at the same time the solar system is installed. Certain plans can bundle this into one project with no large upfront payment.
  • Electrical Upgrade: Older homes often benefit from a panel upgrade or other electrical work. This helps the home safely support modern solar equipment and future electric loads (EV charging, mini-splits, etc.).
  • Tree Removal or Trimming: Strategic tree work can dramatically improve solar production by reducing shade, which can translate into more clean energy from your roof.

The idea is simple: instead of juggling multiple contractors, bids and financing options, you work through one coordinated plan where eligible improvements can be included with $0 upfront, and the cost is recovered over time through your solar PPA or related structure.
